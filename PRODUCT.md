# Product

## Register
**product** — Raisin Console is an authenticated operator dashboard. Design serves the task: send and inspect email, manage domains, audiences, and platform config. (Marketing/landing is secondary, but it presents the console as the product.)

## Users & purpose
Developers and technical founders integrating Raisin (`raisin.run`) into products. Primary jobs: send/debug email, verify domains, manage contacts/topics/suppressions, configure webhooks and API keys, run broadcasts and automations.

## Brand personality
Developer-first, precise, quiet confidence. Near-black Linear-density chrome: Geist throughout, compact 13px UI, hairline borders, list rows instead of metric cards. Orange is reserved for primary actions, the workspace mark, and the current nav icon. Marketing is grayscale with a white pill CTA; the product window is the hero.

## Anti-references
Generic purple AI dashboards; noisy multi-accent admin themes; card-stacked marketing layouts inside the app shell; invented form controls; display serif in product chrome; hero-metric stat cards.

## Accessibility
Prefer WCAG AA contrast on body and controls. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Focus rings on interactive elements.
